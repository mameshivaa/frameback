Quick help

  • Camera unavailable — open Settings, select Frameback, and allow Camera access.
  • Reminders unavailable — open Settings › Notifications › Frameback, and allow notifications.
  • Purchase not recognized — open Frameback's subscription settings and choose Restore Purchases.
  • Missing export — check that the device has enough free storage, then try the export again.

Frameback Pro

Frameback Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, offered monthly and yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and the subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Current prices are shown in the app before you purchase.

The 14-day free trial is separate from the App Store: it costs nothing, needs no payment method, and is tracked on the device itself. Because of that, it does not follow you to another device, and reinstalling the app can reset it.

If a purchase you have already made is not recognized — after reinstalling, or on a new device — open Frameback's subscription settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
