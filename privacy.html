Privacy Policy

Frameback · Last updated September 20, 2026

Frameback is designed to work without an account. Project information, captured photos, thumbnails, and exported films are stored on your device unless you choose to share or save them elsewhere.

Data Frameback accesses

  • Camera — used only when you choose to capture a frame.
  • Photos — used only for photos you select, and for media you explicitly save.
  • Notifications — used only for capture reminders you enable.
  • Purchases — Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an app-generated anonymous identifier to validate the subscription and unlock Frameback Pro. Neither Frameback nor RevenueCat receives your payment-card details.

RevenueCat is the only third-party service integrated in this version. It is used for purchase validation, subscription management, and subscription analytics, not advertising or cross-app tracking. Frameback does not include cloud backup, user accounts, or multi-device synchronization.

Sharing and retention

Frameback does not sell personal data. Photos and project content leave the app only when you initiate a share or save action. Purchase history and an anonymous identifier are processed by Apple and RevenueCat for app functionality and RevenueCat subscription analytics. RevenueCat retains purchase records according to its privacy policy. Deleting a project removes its locally managed media. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backups and any media you previously saved outside the app.

Children

Frameback is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Who is responsible for your data

Frameback is made by Kyoshiro Tajima, in Japan, who is the controller of the data described in this policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to access or delete data, can be sent to 6-accents-helmed@icloud.com. Project media is managed on your device. Requests concerning RevenueCat purchase records can also be made through this address.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and the date above.
