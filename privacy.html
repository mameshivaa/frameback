Privacy Policy

Frameback · Last updated August 21, 2026

Frameback is designed to work without an account. Project information, captured photos, thumbnails, and exported films are stored on your device unless you choose to share or save them elsewhere.

Data Frameback accesses

  • Camera — used only when you choose to capture a frame.
  • Photos — used only for photos you select, and for media you explicitly save.
  • Notifications — used only for capture reminders you enable.
  • Purchases — Apple provides the subscription status and transaction information needed to unlock Frameback Pro. Frameback does not receive your payment-card details.

Frameback does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, cloud backup, user accounts, or multi-device synchronization in this version.

Sharing and retention

Frameback does not sell personal data. Data leaves the app only when you initiate a share or save action, or when Apple processes a purchase. Deleting a project removes its locally managed media. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backups and any media you previously saved outside the app.

Children

Frameback is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to 6-accents-helmed@icloud.com.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and the date above.
